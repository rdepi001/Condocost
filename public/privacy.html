CondoCost Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

CondoCost helps people estimate condo costs and understand association documents. It is operated by Zibblio ("we").

What stays on your device

The properties, numbers, checklist answers and decoded results you enter are saved only in your browser or app storage on your device. We have no account system and do not receive this information. Clearing the app's data deletes it.

Documents you choose to decode (paid)

When you use the Document Decoder, the PDF you choose is sent over an encrypted connection to our server and passed to our AI provider, Anthropic, which reads it and returns the findings. We do not store your documents or the findings on our servers. Anthropic processes the content under its commercial terms, which do not allow it to train models on this data. Upload association documents only; remove pages with personal information such as owner lists or account numbers.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe, which collects your payment details and email. We never see your full card number. We use the Stripe purchase record to unlock your report and to count how many documents it has used.

Other data

Our hosting provider (Netlify) keeps standard server logs, such as IP address and request time, for security and reliability. We do not use advertising trackers and do not sell personal information.

Children

CondoCost is not directed to children under 13.
